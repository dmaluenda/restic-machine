Retention policy

      
restic keeps no policy of its own: it is whatever restic forget is
        given. restic-machine keeps yours, shows here what it would leave, and your backup job
        applies it by running restic-machine forget.

      

        

          Keep the most recent snapshot of each…
          

            
            
            
            
            
            
          

          
For example 7 days keeps the last snapshot of each of the last 7 days that have one. Empty means the rule is not used.

Durations relative to the newest snapshot: y, m, d, h (e.g. 1y6m).
