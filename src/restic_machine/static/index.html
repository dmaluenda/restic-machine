Retention policy

restic keeps no policy of its own: it is whatever restic forget is given. restic-machine keeps yours, shows here what it would leave, and your backup job applies it by running restic-machine forget.

Keep the most recent snapshot of each…

For example 7 days keeps the last snapshot of each of the last 7 days that have one. Empty means the rule is not used.

More rules

Durations relative to the newest snapshot: y, m, d, h (e.g. 1y6m).

Calendar

What the policy leaves alive on a given day, if it keeps being applied every night and the backups go on at their usual times. Each day shows how many of the snapshots made that day are still kept then.

Check with restic

The calendar applies restic's rules itself, to look ahead. Ask restic what this policy would keep and remove right now: a dry run, nothing changes.
